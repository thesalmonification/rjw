2023 Ruben J Williams Foundation Scholar

Dr. Louisa Onyewadume, a 2023 Master's in Public Health candidate at Harvard University, is working to assess and build capacity for spine surgery in Botswana as an RJW Foundation Scholar.

The Christine Awori Hayanga Scholarship

This scholarship, funded by the family of Christine Awori Hayanga, supports a high-achieving African student with financial need at the Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health. Christine Awori-Hayanga was a lawyer and advocate for women's rights.
