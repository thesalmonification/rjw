Impact Statement

Our Commitment to Making a Difference

The RJW Foundation is dedicated to creating lasting positive change in healthcare, especially in resource-limited communities. Our impact is a testament to our unwavering commitment and the support of our partners.

Adapting to Challenges:

The RJW Foundation's 2022 Impact Statement indicates that the COVID-19 pandemic disrupted their activities in 2021. For 2022, the foundation shifted its focus to developing new initiatives and a strategic plan to continue its work. Looking forward to 2023, the foundation plans to leverage technology and implement safety measures to make a significant impact on healthcare in communities with limited resources.

Core Mission

Facilitating healthcare delivery by bringing different groups together.

Sponsoring Future Leaders

Sponsoring aspiring medical students to build future healthcare capacity.

Global Collaboration

Leveraging technology and partnerships for broader impact.

Key Initiatives and Scholarships:

A major initiative for the foundation in 2022/23 is to address and reduce neonatal mortality rates in Kenya. The foundation has also awarded the 2023 Ruben J Williams Scholarship to Dr. Louisa Onyewadume, who is working to improve spine surgery capacity in Botswana. Additionally, the Christine Awori Hayanga Scholarship supports academically gifted African students with financial needs.
