Medical Research

The Ruben J. Williams Foundation is dedicated to advancing medical research in East Africa. The foundation notes that a lack of infrastructure, funding, and updated educational programs are significant obstacles to scientific progress in the region.

Key initiatives of the foundation include:

  • Fostering self-sustaining research teams capable of conducting high-quality health studies in Africa.
  • Advocating for increased investment in African science to address diseases affecting the continent and the world.
  • Addressing the disparity in representation for African researchers in the global scientific community.

The foundation believes that improving the compensation of African scientists is crucial for retaining talent and enhancing research results.
