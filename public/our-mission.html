Our Mission

The Ruben J. Williams Foundation is dedicated to addressing disparities in surgical and critical healthcare in developing countries, particularly in Eastern Africa. Our mission is driven by a commitment to global health equity.

Bridging the Healthcare Gap:

Founded in 2005, our primary goal is to address disparities in surgical and critical healthcare. We connect academic organizations and hospitals in the US with institutions in East Africa to facilitate specialist training.

Global Exchange of Expertise:

Our mission is to create a global exchange of ideas, advances, and skills in the medical field. We aim to positively impact the training of surgeons and anesthesiologists in East Africa through short-term academic visits.

Our Goals:

  • Establish an academic surgical/anesthesiology professional network.
  • Enhance surgical training and practice in Africa.
  • Offer unique cultural and professional exchange for visiting surgeons.
  • Focus on curriculum design, enhancement, and research networks.

Long-Term Vision:

The ultimate goal is to establish an academic surgical/anesthesiology professional network that encourages the free flow of academic thought, research, and personnel. This initiative aims to enhance surgical training and practice in Africa while offering a unique cultural and professional exchange for visiting surgeons. The foundation also focuses on curriculum design, enhancement, and establishing an international lecture and research network in surgery, critical care, and nursing.
