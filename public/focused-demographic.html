Focused Demographic

Current Healthcare Challenges:

Health care in Africa is described as the worst in the world, with 50% of health expenditure being out-of-pocket. The region lacks infrastructure and trained medical personnel. Access to modern health facilities is limited to fewer than 50% of Africans, and many countries spend less than 10% of their GDP on healthcare. In Kenya, there is one doctor for every 5,000 people, and in Uganda, public hospitals are often overcrowded and under-stocked.

What RJW Foundation Can do for Healthcare:

The RJW Foundation aims to strengthen the capacity of African institutions to conduct internationally competitive research. The foundation focuses on improving maternal and child health, surgical/critical care, chronic diseases, and building resilient health systems. It also seeks to inspire future generations to be responsible for others and to collaborate.
